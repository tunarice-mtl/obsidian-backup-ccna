


- Don’t wing stuff. Admitting when you don’t know something and finding someone who _does_ know
- Always attend to customer first
- Personality, relatable to customers
- Ask the customer lots of questions. Not only helps you figure out what the problem is, but it also makes the customer feel better because they know that you’re listening to them.
